# Emotional expression in generated images

Emotional expression concerns how well an image conveys a target feeling. It is distinct from whether viewers find the image attractive and from whether all details of an emotional situation are depicted.

## Evidence in this wiki

[[RePrompt]] studies natural emotional situation descriptions and edits them using noun/verb reduction, concrete adjective additions, and an appended emotion label. The human evaluation finds RePrompt’s rating advantages over other methods mainly for negative emotions. Overall practical rating differences are small, and rankings show no significant condition differences.

CLIP emotion scores do not have uniform human correspondence: on Emotion6, correlations range from .6117 for sadness to .0761 for joy and −.1586 for surprise. These results caution against treating label-similarity gains as equally meaningful across emotions. They do not by themselves establish a universal psychological difference between positive and negative emotion perception.

## Thesis implications

Analyze target categories separately, validate emotion metrics against human judgments, and retain a context-preservation outcome. The source motivates emotional-image applications but does not test therapeutic or wellbeing benefits.
